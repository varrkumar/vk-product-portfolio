Entry 03

The question I was handed

Why is the sharing interface confusing?

The question it turned out to be

Why won’t scientists share an idea before it’s finished?

Lead product designer.
14 weeks, spring 2024.
Two engineers, one researcher, & a very patient PM.
(this reframe is the whole story. everything below is how I got here.)
01
the brief

Lab Notebook is a research tool used by about 400 scientists. Its sharing feature was barely used: people opened the share dialog 1.2 times on average, then closed it without sending anything.

The hypothesis I was given: the interface is confusing. Fix the dialog.

Opening it and closing it isn’t confusion. Confused people file tickets. We had almost none.
02
listening

I ran six contextual interviews, sitting beside researchers while they worked. Nobody struggled with the dialog. They understood it fine. They just didn’t want to press the button.

Six is small. I knew it. But it was enough to show the question was wrong.
3 of 6 said “it’s not ready yet” without being asked.
03
the turn

The interface wasn’t the barrier. The social contract was. Sharing meant committing to an opinion in front of colleagues.

Validated in 6 of 6 interviews Observed in usage logs Assumed: true outside this lab

The honest part: senior researchers barely changed. Drafts worked for people who felt they had something to lose. I hadn’t planned for the people who didn’t.

Next time I’d interview the people who already share, not just the ones who don’t. The answer was hiding with them.
